Showtime Cinemas

Consumer analytics · team of 7
Results

Three growth plays from booking data

Showtime Cinemas, a major Taiwanese cinema chain, asked our club’s 7-person consulting team to grow its anime box office. Using its booking data, we found what drives sales and turned it into strategy.

Method

From messy data to two models, step by step

How I did the analysis in Python, in five steps.

Swipe to compare →
01Collect
  • Booking records for the chain’s anime films
  • Public box-office data
  • Ticket price sheets
  • Campaign calendars
02Clean in Python
  • Merged the per-film files into one table (pandas)
  • One column per ticket type and snack
  • Tagged each order with the bonus items on offer
  • Kept paid orders only
03Check quality
  • Many member profiles had missing or default values
  • So customer profiles were unreliable
04Model
  • K-means (scikit-learn): group the cinemas
  • Poisson regression (statsmodels): bonus items → tickets
  • t-tests: which bundle contents sell
05Recommend
  • Split box office into price × volume
  • 3 strategy directions, each with a projected outcome
Key decision at step 3The data could not describe customers reliably, so we compared the cinemas instead.
