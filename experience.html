Compliance

PIF launch-readiness tracker

Taiwan's new cosmetics regulation requires a Product Information File (PIF) for each product before launch. Each PIF covers many SKUs and moves through steps owned by different teams, each with its own lead time.

With the Compliance team, I built a VBA tracker that records the date each step is completed and updates the forecast finish date for every PIF. A summary view compares the forecast with the required date and flags delays by severity.

Result: 40% better workflow visibility across 200+ SKUs.
